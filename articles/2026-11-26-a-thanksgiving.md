*Dnes je v USA Thanksgiving – Deň vďakyvzdania. Čo je to za sviatok a ako ho Američania oslavujú?*

Today is Thanksgiving in the United States. It is a big **holiday**, and for many Americans it is more important than New Year's Day. Thanksgiving is always on the fourth Thursday in November.

On Thanksgiving, families come together. Some people **travel** a long way to see their parents and grandparents. The airports and the roads are very busy. It is one of the busiest travel times of the year.

The most important part of the day is a big dinner. The main dish is a **turkey**. A turkey is a large bird, and one turkey can feed ten or more people. Next to the turkey, there are mashed potatoes, gravy (a warm brown sauce), and sweet cranberry sauce. For dessert, many families eat pumpkin **pie**.

Before dinner, some families do a special thing. Every person at the table says what they are **thankful** for. Some people are thankful for their family, their health, or their friends. That is the idea of the holiday: to say "thank you" for the good things in life.

Food is not the only tradition. In New York City, there is a big **parade** every Thanksgiving morning, with music and huge balloons. Millions of people watch it on TV. In the afternoon, many Americans watch football. It is American football, not soccer!

The holiday is very old. It goes back to the 1600s and the first English families in America. But for most people today, Thanksgiving is simply a day for family, food, and thanks.

### New words

- **holiday** – sviatok (aj dovolenka)
- **travel** – cestovať
- **turkey** – moriak, morka
- **pie** – koláč (plnený, napr. tekvicový alebo jablkový)
- **thankful** – vďačný
- **parade** – sprievod, prehliadka

### Useful phrases

- *come together* – stretnúť sa, zísť sa
- *the main dish* – hlavné jedlo
- *be thankful for…* – byť vďačný za…
- *It goes back to…* – Siaha to do… (o histórii)
- *soccer* – futbal (Američania hovoria *soccer*, lebo *football* je u nich americký futbal)

### Grammar

**Prítomný čas (present simple)** používame na veci, ktoré sa dejú pravidelne alebo stále platia:
*Thanksgiving is always on the fourth Thursday. Many families eat pumpkin pie.*
Často s ním idú slová *always* (vždy), *often* (často), *every* (každý).
