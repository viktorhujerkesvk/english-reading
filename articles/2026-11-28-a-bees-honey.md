*Ako včely vyrábajú med a prečo ho v úli majú toľko?*

A honeybee is a small insect, but it does a very big job. Bees **collect** nectar, a sweet liquid inside flowers. They make honey from it.

A bee drinks nectar from a flower and keeps it in a special "honey stomach." Then it flies back to the **hive**, its home. There, it gives the nectar to other bees. They chew it and pass it from bee to bee. Then they put it into small wax cells. The bees move their **wings** very fast, and this dries the nectar. Slowly, it turns into thick, sweet honey.

Bees work very hard. To make one **jar** of honey (about 450 grams), they visit about two million flowers. One bee makes only about one-twelfth of a teaspoon of honey in its whole life.

Why do bees make so much honey? It is their food for the winter. In the cold months, there are no flowers. The bees stay inside the hive, eat honey, and keep each other warm. **Beekeepers** take only the extra honey and leave enough for the bees.

Bees are also important for people. When a bee visits flowers, it **carries** pollen from one flower to another. This helps plants make fruit and seeds. Without bees, we have fewer apples, cherries, and many other foods.

### New words

- **collect** – zbierať
- **hive** – úľ
- **wing** / wings – krídlo / krídla
- **jar** – pohár (so šraubovacím viečkom), zaváraninová fľaša
- **beekeeper** – včelár
- **carry** – niesť, nosiť

### Useful phrases

- *turn into…* – zmeniť sa na…
- *work hard* – tvrdo pracovať
- *keep each other warm* – zohrievať sa navzájom
- *from bee to bee* – od včely k včele

### Grammar

**He / she / it + -s.** V prítomnom čase pri jednom (he, she, it) pridávame k slovesu -s:
*A bee drinks nectar.* – ale – *Bees drink nectar.*
*It flies back to the hive.* (fly → flies, lebo slovo končí na -y)
