title: How a Rainbow Works
publish: 2026-11-29 17:00

*Kedy a prečo vzniká dúha – a prečo k nej nikdy nedôjdeš.*

You need two things for a rainbow: sun and rain. The best time is right after a rain **shower**, when the sun comes out again.

Remember: sunlight is a mix of all colors. When sunlight goes into a drop of water, the light **bends**. Every color bends a little differently. Red bends the least, and violet bends the most. Inside the drop, the light reflects, like in a mirror, and then it comes out again. Now the colors are **separate**, and we see them in the sky: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet.

To see a rainbow, the sun must be **behind** you, and the rain must be in front of you. That is why we often see rainbows in the morning or in the late afternoon, when the sun is low. At noon, the sun is too high, and you usually don't see a rainbow.

Sometimes there are two rainbows in the sky. The second one is lighter, and its colors are in the **opposite** order: red is on the inside, not on the outside.

A rainbow looks like a **bridge**, but you can never walk to it. When you move, the rainbow moves with you. In fact, two people next to each other see two slightly different rainbows. Every person has their own rainbow!

### New words

- **shower** – prehánka (aj sprcha)
- **bend** – ohnúť sa, lámať sa (o svetle)
- **separate** – oddelený
- **behind** – za (niečím)
- **opposite** – opačný
- **bridge** – most

### Useful phrases

- *right after…* – hneď po…
- *the sun comes out* – vyjde slnko (spoza mrakov)
- *in front of you / behind you* – pred tebou / za tebou
- *next to each other* – vedľa seba
- *in fact* – v skutočnosti
