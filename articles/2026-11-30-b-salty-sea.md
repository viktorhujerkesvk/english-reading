---
title: Why Is the Sea Salty?
publish: 2026-11-30 13:00
---

*Rieky majú sladkú vodu a všetky tečú do mora. Prečo je teda more slané?*

Rivers have fresh water. It is not **salty**. But rivers go into the sea, and the sea is salty. How is this possible?

The story starts with rain. Rain falls on mountains and on the ground. On its way to the river, the water touches rocks and **soil**. It takes very small amounts of salt from them. River water has this salt too, but only very little, so it doesn't taste salty.

Rivers carry this salt to the sea. In the sea, the sun warms the water, and some of it **evaporates** – it goes up into the air and makes clouds. But the salt can't go up with the water. It stays in the sea. This happens again and again for millions of years. Slowly, the sea gets saltier and saltier.

How salty is the sea? In one liter of sea water, there are about 35 grams of salt. That is about two big **spoons** of salt. You can't drink it. Salt water makes you more **thirsty**, not less.

Some seas are saltier than others. The Dead Sea, between Israel and Jordan, is almost ten times saltier than the ocean. The water is so heavy with salt that people float on it easily. You can lie on the water and read a newspaper!

### New words

- **salty** – slaný
- **soil** – pôda, zemina
- **evaporate** – vyparovať sa
- **spoon** – lyžica
- **thirsty** – smädný

### Useful phrases

- *fresh water* – sladká voda (nie slaná)
- *on its way to…* – na ceste do…
- *again and again* – znova a znova
- *saltier and saltier* – stále slanejší

### Grammar

**Porovnávanie: -er / more.** Krátke prídavné mená dostanú -er:
*salty → saltier, heavy → heavier, cold → colder, quiet → quieter*
Dlhšie slová dostanú *more*: *more important, more interesting*.
Za porovnaním ide *than* (ako): *The Dead Sea is saltier than the ocean.*

### Review

Slová, ktoré už poznáš: **carry**, **float**, **heavy**, **clouds**
