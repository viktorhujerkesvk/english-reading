*Čo je blesk, prečo hrmí a ako jednoducho zistíš, ako ďaleko je búrka.*

A **storm** is one of the most exciting things in nature. First you see a bright **flash** of **lightning**. A few seconds later, you hear the loud sound of **thunder**.

Lightning is electricity. Inside a big storm cloud, tiny pieces of ice move up and down very fast. They hit each other, and this makes electricity. When there is a lot of electricity, it jumps from the cloud to the ground, or to another cloud. That jump is lightning.

Lightning is extremely hot – about five times hotter than the sun's surface. It heats the air around it in a moment. The hot air gets bigger very fast, like an explosion. That is the sound of thunder.

Light travels much faster than sound. That is why we see the lightning first and hear the thunder later. You can use this to know how far away a storm is. **Count** the seconds between the flash and the thunder. Divide the number by three. That is the **distance** in kilometers. For example, nine seconds means the storm is about three kilometers away.

Is it safe outside? Not really. If you hear thunder, the storm is close enough for lightning to hit you. Go inside a building or a car. Don't stand under a tall tree. Americans have an easy rule: "When thunder roars, go indoors."

### New words

- **storm** – búrka
- **flash** – záblesk
- **lightning** – blesk
- **thunder** – hrom, hrmenie
- **count** – počítať
- **distance** – vzdialenosť

### Useful phrases

- *how far away* – ako ďaleko
- *close enough* – dosť blízko
- *in a moment* – za okamih
- *When thunder roars, go indoors.* – Keď hrmí, choď dnu.
