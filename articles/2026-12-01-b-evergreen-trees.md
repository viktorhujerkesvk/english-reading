title: Why Evergreen Trees Stay Green
publish: 2026-12-01 17:00

*Prečo ihličnany v zime neopadajú a odkiaľ pochádza vianočný stromček?*

In the fall, many trees lose their leaves. But some trees stay green all winter. In English, we call them **evergreen** trees. **Pines**, spruces, and firs are evergreens. They have **needles**, not **wide** leaves.

Why don't they lose their needles in winter? Needles are small and **narrow**, and they have a thick, waxy skin. This skin keeps water inside. In winter, the ground is **frozen**, and it is hard for trees to get water. Wide leaves lose a lot of water, so many trees drop them. Needles lose very little water, so evergreens can keep them.

Evergreens lose needles too, but slowly. A needle stays on the tree for a few years. Then it falls, and a new one grows. That is why the ground in a pine forest is always soft and brown.

The shape of an evergreen tree is also smart. Many of them look like a triangle: wide at the bottom and narrow at the top. Heavy snow slides down the branches and doesn't break them.

In December, evergreens are in many homes. The Christmas tree tradition comes from Germany. Today, Americans buy about 25 to 30 million real Christmas trees every year. Many families go to a Christmas tree farm and cut their own tree. Others buy an artificial tree and use it again every year.

### New words

- **evergreen** – vždyzelený (strom)
- **pine** – borovica
- **needle** / needles – ihlica / ihličie (aj ihla)
- **wide** – široký
- **narrow** – úzky
- **frozen** – zamrznutý

### Useful phrases

- *lose leaves* – strácať lístie
- *stay green* – zostať zelený
- *at the bottom / at the top* – dole / hore
- *a Christmas tree farm* – farma, kde si sám vyberieš a zotneš stromček
