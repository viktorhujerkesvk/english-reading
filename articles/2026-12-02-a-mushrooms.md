*Huby nie sú rastliny ani zvieratá. Čo teda sú a čo robia v lese pod zemou?*

Many people in Slovakia love to **pick** mushrooms in the forest. But what is a mushroom? It is not a plant and not an animal. Mushrooms **belong** to their own group, called fungi.

The mushroom we pick is only a small part of the fungus. The biggest part lives under the ground. It is a large network of very thin white **threads**. We usually can't see it. The mushroom is like an apple on an apple tree. It is the part that makes spores – tiny "seeds" that the wind carries to new places.

Plants make their own food from sunlight. Mushrooms can't do that. They get food from dead leaves, old wood, and other things in the soil. In this way, they clean the forest and help make new soil. Without fungi, the forest is full of dead leaves and old trees.

Many fungi are friends of trees. Their threads connect with tree **roots** under the ground. The fungus helps the tree get water, and the tree gives the fungus sugar. Some scientists even say that trees "talk" to each other through this network.

Some fungi are huge. In Oregon, in the U.S., there is one fungus under a forest that covers almost ten square kilometers. It is one of the biggest living things on Earth.

But be **careful**: some mushrooms are very **poisonous**. Never eat a mushroom if you are not 100 percent sure what it is.

### New words

- **pick** – zbierať, trhať (huby, ovocie)
- **belong** – patriť
- **thread** / threads – vlákno / vlákna (aj niť)
- **root** / roots – koreň / korene
- **careful** – opatrný
- **poisonous** – jedovatý

### Useful phrases

- *pick mushrooms* – zbierať huby
- *under the ground* – pod zemou
- *be careful* – dávaj pozor
- *be sure* – byť si istý
